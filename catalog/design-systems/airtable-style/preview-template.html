Tokens · Surfaces · Five polarities

Five polarities, one rhythm.

Editorial pages rotate through white → cream → coral → forest → navy. The canvas resets between every signature surface so two of the same polarity never sit next to each other.

Canvas --background editorial body · default page floor
Callout --brand-signature-cream soft beige interlude · stat callouts
Signature --brand-signature-coral biggest brand moment · once per scroll
Signature --brand-signature-forest deep green sibling to coral
Voltage --brand-surface-dark dark navy CTA card · mid-page

The path to 10× every person in the operating team.

Halcyon's product team ships ten times more weekly specs since the workspace moved here. The dark navy card is the brand's voltage moment — never decorative, always a recommitment.
