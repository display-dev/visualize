Position statement

The artifact is the product.

Everything else — accounts, dashboards, billing, the entire CRUD of a SaaS product — is overhead. The artifact is the only thing that matters.

01

One published link.

The unit of delivery is a URL. Not a doc. Not a Slack message. Not a PDF in an email. A URL that loads.

02

Behind the wall.

Public is the lazy default. Org-gated is the harder, more honest one — most artifacts shouldn't be on the open web.

03

Cheap or free.

Pricing matches what the cloud actually costs. Anything more is selling overhead as if it were product.

If your artifact takes longer to share than it took to make, the tool got in the way.
