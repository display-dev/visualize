Tokens · Palette

Black, white, and the air between.

The palette is the absence of palette. Near-pure black canvas; the only other surfaces are progressive elevation steps in nearly-black gray. There is no accent colour, no chrome, no decorative tint. Type and whitespace carry the entire system.

Tokens · Surfaces

Three steps, no chrome.

Tokens · Type + components

Three families, modest weights.

Bugatti Display for headlines, Bugatti Text Regular for body, Bugatti Monospace for engineering numerals. Modest weights throughout — display at 300, body at 300, label at 400. Letter-spacing runs wide on uppercase (0.24–0.32em) to feel European-engineered.
