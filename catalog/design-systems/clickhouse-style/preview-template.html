Tokens · Surfaces

Four surfaces, near-black ladder.

Canvas at oklch(0.14), secondary at 0.18, card at 0.22, elevated at 0.26. Color-block contrast is deliberately subtle — depth comes from hairline borders and the yellow CTAs, not from canvas tone shifts.
