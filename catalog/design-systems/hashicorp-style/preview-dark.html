Tokens · Surfaces

Four near-black registers.

Pure-black canvas as the page floor. Three lifted charcoal surfaces with slight blue tint for cards, code blocks, and inputs. Surfaces stack subtly — depth comes from hairline borders and per-product accents, not from luminance jumps.
