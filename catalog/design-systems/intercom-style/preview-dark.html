Tokens · Surfaces · Five steps

Cream up, charcoal down.

Pages rotate through cream canvas, white cards, a softer cream alt-row, hairline-bordered floating tiles, and a deep inverse for the testimonial strip. The inverse stays locked at near-black on both polarities — it's the brand's permanent dark-context register, not a theme variant.

Tokens · Type + components

Saans weight 500, measured negative tracking.

Display headlines run Inter 500 with -0.035em tracking (the open-source approximation of Saans weight 500 + the documented -2.0px scale). Body type stays Inter 400, sentence case eyebrows, no all-caps tracking — the brand resists the marketing-typography reflex.

Tokens · FAQ

Hairline accordion, no chrome.

A documented brand pattern: questions stack on the cream canvas itself, separated only by a soft hairline rule. No card lift, no chevron decoration, no accent — the type does the work.

How does the AI co-pilot decide when to draft a reply? −

It reads the customer's most recent message, the thread history, and the help-doc corpus you've connected. When a confident first draft is available the co-pilot offers it inline to the assigned teammate — the human always sends.
