Tokens · Surfaces · Five steps

White canvas through dark, two pastel tints between.

The page rotates through canvas, surface, the yellow sticky-tag tint, the lavender featured-tier tint, and the dark footer black. The dark step is scarce by design — used for the footer and the dark CTA banner, never as a section fill.

01 · Canvas --background page floor · board surface

05 · Dark --brand-footer-bg footer · CTA banner · scarce voltage

Tokens · Type + components

Roobert PRO across every surface.

Display headlines run Roobert PRO 500 with -0.04em tracking. The face stays the same from the 80px hero down to 11px footer microcopy; only weight (400 body, 500 medium, 600 caption-bold) and tracking shift. No serif, no second display face.
