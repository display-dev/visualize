How the team works

Three rituals, one queue.

The card register — hairline border, green corner-square, 2px geometry, no shadow. The whole system reads as engineering documentation that learned graphic design.

Reservation lanes

Production inference, training runs, and exploratory notebooks each queue against their own reserved share. No noisy-neighbour conflicts across teams.

Step throughput

A single weekly dashboard tracks step-per-second against checkpoint cost. The ML engineer sees the same numbers the finance team sees the next morning.

Window pricing

Off-peak hours land at a documented discount. Long training runs schedule against the curve; nightly batch jobs ride the dip without anyone hand-tuning the cron.

Pricing

Three tiers, one inversion.

The featured tier flips to the documented dark surface — colour-block contrast does the elevation work. No accent borders, no scale shifts, no badge. The frame chapter holds the voltage.
