Warm cream chrome, scarce red, never decorative.

Tokens · Surfaces · Five steps

White through warm near-black, three creams between.

The page rotates through canvas (true white), soft (faint cream), card (warm cream), hairline (gray-cream), and the scarce dark voltage. The dark step is reserved for the rare CTA strip and the modal scrim — never as a section fill.

01 · Canvas --background page floor · pin tile · modal surface
02 · Soft --secondary hero wash · alternating band
03 · Cream --card search pill rest · category tile fill
04 · Hairline tier --brand-secondary-bg secondary button · row divider
05 · Dark voltage --brand-surface-dark CTA strip · modal scrim ink

Tokens · Type + components

Pin Sans across every role, tight tracking at display.

Display headlines run heavy at 600 weight with negative tracking (-1.2px at 70px) so the type feels confident rather than airy. Body sits at 16 / 1.4. There is no serif and no monospace in the chrome — the entire system runs on a single proprietary geometric sans (Pin Sans, with Inter as the documented open-source substitute).
