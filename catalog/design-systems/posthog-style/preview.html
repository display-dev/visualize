Tokens · Surfaces

Three surface registers.

Cream canvas as the page floor. White cards lifted with a 1.5px charcoal border and a 2-3px offset shadow. A slightly off-white doc surface for tree-card marginalia. No drop shadows — hairlines and ink contrast carry the depth.
