Tokens · Surfaces · Two polarities

Four surfaces, two polarities.

Marketing surfaces sit on the violet midnight canvas with night cards. Pricing and dense reference flip to white canvas with cloud-hairline cards. The system never blurs the two — only the polarity rotates.
