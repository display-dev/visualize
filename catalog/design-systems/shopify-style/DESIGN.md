# Shopify Style

_Independent reference inspired by Shopify's public product and marketing surfaces. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Shopify._

## §1 Canonical Canvas

Shopify style is a commerce operating system with a high-energy AI-forward marketing layer and a clear merchant-utility layer. The current homepage leads with "Be the next AI all-star," "Dream big and build fast," and selling everywhere people shop: online, in person, across AI, social, local, and global channels. This is not just an online store builder style anymore. It is an agentic commerce platform style.

Use bright merchant imagery, product screenshots, checkout modules, green commerce accents, black pill actions, cream or white utility panels, and confident headlines that speak to ambition. The system should make merchants feel like scale is reachable tomorrow, while making operators trust the checkout, payments, orders, inventory, shipping, analytics, and app ecosystem underneath.

### global-header

Use a substantial commerce nav with Why Shopify, Products, Pricing, Enterprise, Start for free, and Log in. Product menus group around Build your website, Sell anywhere, Marketing and analytics, Run your business, Get paid, Customize and extend, and Non-stop innovation. Keep the header crisp and conversion-oriented.

### ai-all-star-hero

The hero uses a rotating-identity headline pattern: AI all-star, household name, solo-preneur, category creator, global empire, store they line up for, big thing. Keep the headline huge, direct, and optimistic. Pair with one primary "Start for free" pill.

### merchant-store-gallery

Use real-feeling storefront tiles as proof: product photography, brand names, URLs, and checkout buttons. Tiles can overlap, scroll, or form a collage, but every image should look like a merchant store, not abstract commerce art.

### agentic-storefront-panel

Agentic Storefronts show products discovered in AI channels, checkout inside chat, and orders tracked in Shopify. The composition should include a chat surface, product cards, a checkout confirmation, and admin order status. It must feel like a commerce channel, not a generic assistant.

### sidekick-ai-card

Sidekick is the commerce AI assistant built into admin. Show a merchant asking for pricing analysis, product recommendations, campaign ideas, or task automation. The answer should use store data and end in an action.

### sell-everywhere-grid

Represent online store, AI chats, point of sale, Shop App, social, marketplaces, global, B2B, and Markets as channels in a grid. Each tile needs a practical commerce object: product, buyer, order, region, payment, or inventory.

### checkout-proof-card

Checkout proof cards should emphasize conversion, Shop Pay, high-intent shoppers, fast checkout, and trust. Use a compact checkout UI with payment method, shipping, total, and "Pay now." Include a stat panel when relevant.

### shop-pay-module

Shop Pay modules are rounded, high-contrast, and extremely direct: saved buyer, shipping address, payment method, delivery estimate, and call to pay. Avoid turning checkout into a decorative form.

### build-fast-steps

Use a three-step launch strip: add your first product, customize your store, set up payments. Numbering is large and simple. The strip should feel achievable, not bureaucratic.

### product-admin-card

Admin cards expose title, inventory, price, channel availability, variants, and status. Keep them clean, with green positive states and black text. The admin is where confidence comes from.

### orders-inventory-card

Operational cards show orders, fulfillment, inventory alerts, return states, and shipping labels. Use small badges, totals, and clear next actions. Commerce work is repetitive, so these components need scan speed.

### analytics-growth-card

Analytics cards show sales, conversion, average order value, returning customers, marketing performance, and channel mix. Use green upward states sparingly and neutral charts for the rest.

### capital-card

Shopify Capital surfaces use money, repayment, eligibility, and no-equity messaging. Keep the card trustworthy, not flashy. Use clear numbers, terms, and a merchant quote.

### app-store-card

App ecosystem cards highlight categories, ratings, install counts, and integrations. The App Store is large, so use search, filters, and compact cards rather than decorative app bubbles.

### theme-store-card

Theme cards show desktop and mobile storefront previews, price, style tags, and conversion-oriented attributes. Store design is visual; use actual storefront frames.

### developer-commerce-card

Developer cards cover Shopify.dev, Hydrogen, APIs, CLI, Commerce for Agents, Universal Commerce Protocol, and MCP support. Use code snippets, API cards, and integration diagrams with commerce outcomes attached.

### global-markets-map

Markets surfaces should show countries, currencies, duties, shipping, localization, and region-specific storefronts. The map is useful only when paired with commerce controls.

### b2b-commerce-card

B2B cards show company profiles, price lists, payment terms, catalogs, purchase orders, and buyer roles. The style stays premium but operational.

### pos-card

POS cards connect in-person checkout with inventory, customer profiles, and online order history. Use device frames, receipt totals, and staff actions.

### social-marketplace-card

Social and marketplace cards show product syndication, campaign status, orders from channels, and performance. Keep social logos secondary to the merchant product.

### pricing-plan-card

Pricing cards are clear white or cream panels with plan name, monthly price, primary fit, selling features, staff or user limits where relevant, and Start trial action. Highlight plan differences without hiding fees or commerce limits.

### comparison-table

Comparison tables must stay readable: plan columns, features rows, payments, checkout, shipping, inventory, analytics, automation, markets, B2B, support, and app capabilities. Use sticky headers and understated green checks.

### enterprise-plus-panel

Enterprise surfaces should feel stable and ambitious: global scale, checkout extensibility, B2B, automation, integrations, and dedicated support. Use larger photography and denser operational proof.

### merchant-story-panel

Merchant stories pair a founder or brand image with revenue, growth, launch, or expansion detail. Keep the story concrete: what they sold, where they started, how Shopify helped them scale.

### ecosystem-footer

Footers are large international commerce directories: Shopify, Ecosystem, Resources, Support, country and language selector. The footer can be dense because Shopify is global.

## §2 Palette

Shopify's palette is commerce green plus black/white/cream foundations. Green signals growth and Shopify identity; black gives the hero and pills weight; cream softens long-form commerce content.

#### green-commerce

Use a vivid Shopify green for brand marks, growth highlights, positive states, and selected channel accents. It should appear confident, not neon.

#### black-pill

Use black or near-black filled pills for the primary CTA on light surfaces. On dark hero surfaces, white pills can take the primary role.

#### cream-canvas

Use cream or pale mint backgrounds for pricing, comparison, education, and admin-adjacent pages. Keep them light and clean.

#### merchant-color

Let merchant photography and storefront screenshots provide most of the secondary color. Do not create a rainbow UI palette around Shopify green.

## §3 Typography

Shopify type is large, confident, and plainspoken. Headings can be massive; admin and pricing copy must remain practical.

#### display-headline

Use large grotesk display type with tight line-height and natural sentence-case rhythm. Avoid luxury thinness that makes the brand feel less entrepreneurial.

#### body-copy

Use a clean sans for body and UI. Copy should be short, active, and merchant-centered: sell, build, ship, get paid, grow, automate.

#### number-type

Stats need large, readable numbers with compact labels. Percentages, shopper counts, loan amounts, and order totals are commerce proof.

## §4 Composition Rules

Every composition should connect a merchant ambition to commerce infrastructure. The top layer says "build fast"; the lower layer proves checkout, inventory, payments, channels, and admin can handle it.

### proof-before-abstraction

Use storefronts, checkout, product cards, orders, and admin before abstract diagrams. Commerce is concrete.

### one-growth-message

Each section should state one growth message: sell everywhere, launch fast, convert better, manage globally, automate with AI, or extend with apps.

### channel-continuity

When showing multiple channels, connect them back to a single Shopify order/admin view. This is the platform promise.

### photography-with-ui

Photography should be paired with UI overlays or store frames. Pure lifestyle imagery is weaker than merchant proof.

### mobile-commerce-first

Many surfaces should include mobile storefront and checkout views. Buyers shop on phones; merchants manage on phones too.

## §5 Accessibility And States

Commerce interfaces must be clear under pressure. Buttons, totals, plan prices, checkout actions, and order states need strong contrast and explicit labels.

### checkout-focus

Focus states around checkout and payment controls must be visible and calm. Use green or black outlines with enough offset.

### positive-state

Positive states use green plus text: paid, fulfilled, active, connected, synced, growing. Never rely on green alone.

### risk-state

Inventory warnings, failed payments, fraud review, and shipping problems use clear labels and amber or red accents separate from brand green.

### loading-state

Loading states should look like product grids, order rows, or dashboard cards. Avoid generic spinners on checkout-critical surfaces.

## §6 Anti-Patterns

Shopify style fails when it becomes a generic green SaaS page or when it forgets actual merchants.

### avoid-empty-green-gradients

Green gradients without products, stores, orders, or checkout proof feel off-brand.

### avoid-b2b-blandness

Enterprise Shopify still needs merchant energy. Do not turn Plus into a generic procurement page.

### avoid-fake-commerce

Every product, order, price, and checkout module should look plausible. Fake lorem commerce breaks trust quickly.

### avoid-ai-as-decoration

Sidekick and agentic commerce must do commerce work. Do not use sparkle icons as the main AI cue.
