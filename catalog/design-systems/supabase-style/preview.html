Tokens · Surfaces

Three surface registers.

White canvas for the page floor. Barely-tinted off-white for alternating section bands. Deep near-black canvas-night for code blocks, the dashboard editor pane, and the featured pricing tier. Depth comes from hairlines and a single Level-2 shadow on floating product mockups.
