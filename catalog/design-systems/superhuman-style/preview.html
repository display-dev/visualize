Tokens · Surfaces · Four polarities

Four surfaces, two darks.

White and canvas-soft for body. Indigo for the hero and any inset feature card. Teal for the closing CTA band — a different warm-dark from the indigo, the brand's deliberate two-dark register.

Tokens · Type + components

Super Sans at 460/540, tight tracking.

A single proprietary variable display sans at modest mid-weights — 460 for body, 540 for display. Tracking runs aggressively negative on display sizes. The brand reads more like a high-end newsletter than a SaaS app.
