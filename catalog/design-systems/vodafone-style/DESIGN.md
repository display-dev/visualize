# Vodafone Style

_Independent reference inspired by Vodafone's public consumer and business surfaces. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Vodafone._

## §1 Canonical Canvas

Vodafone style is bold connectivity: mobile plans, broadband, 5G, Vodafone Together bundles, devices, rewards, business networks, IoT, private mobile networks, and 5G slicing. The design speaks in red, white, black, oversized type, and direct offers. It is a telecom retail system with enterprise network proof underneath.

Use Vodafone red as the signature action and campaign color. Use white for plan comparison and support. Use black or dark image bands for high-impact campaigns. Photography and device renders can be large, but plan details, prices, speeds, contract terms, and network conditions must stay legible.

### global-header

Use a clear telecom nav: Shop, Mobile, Broadband, TV, Deals, Business, Support, My Vodafone, Basket. Keep account and basket visible. Consumer surfaces need fast shopping paths.

### red-campaign-hero

The hero can use a red band, black image band, or white offer stage. Headline is big and direct: mobile, broadband, 5G, switch, save, or bundle. One primary CTA dominates.

### mobile-plan-card

Mobile plan cards show data allowance, monthly price, contract length, upfront cost, roaming, speed tier, phone eligibility, and price-change note. Keep terms visible.

### device-offer-card

Device cards show phone image, storage, color, monthly cost, upfront cost, trade-in, upgrade, and plan pairing. Use large product renders and clean pricing rows.

### sim-only-card

SIM-only cards show data, minutes/texts, speed, roaming, monthly cost, and contract flexibility. These need dense but readable comparison.

### broadband-plan-card

Broadband cards show speed, monthly cost, contract, router, WiFi guarantee, installation, and bill credit or bundle discount. Use speed as the hero number.

### five-g-broadband-card

5G Broadband cards show self-install, 5G hub, monthly or 24-month plan, covered homes, no landline, and speed estimate. Make fixed wireless feel practical.

### vodafone-together-card

Together bundles show mobile plus broadband benefits, additional plan discounts, shared account, Secure Net, and family controls. Use a paired-card structure.

### coverage-checker-card

Coverage checker cards show postcode input, 4G/5G/fibre availability, indoor/outdoor status, and next action. This is a core telecom utility.

### roaming-card

Roaming cards show destination, zone, included roaming, daily charge, fair use, and plan eligibility. Make international terms clear.

### veryme-rewards-card

Rewards cards show offer, partner, expiry, redemption action, and customer eligibility. Use red sparingly with brand partner imagery.

### secure-net-card

Secure Net and parental control cards show protection, blocked threats, family controls, setup, and trial period. Keep safety clear.

### my-vodafone-card

App account cards show plan, data used, bill, add-ons, upgrade, rewards, support, and usage. Mobile-first layout matters.

### bill-breakdown-card

Bill cards show monthly plan, device payment, add-ons, out-of-bundle charges, discounts, VAT, and annual price changes. Use itemized rows.

### support-flow-card

Support cards show topic, account state, diagnostic step, live chat, repair, or store appointment. Telecom support needs specificity.

### network-status-card

Network cards show region, service, planned work, outage, 4G/5G status, and expected fix. Use explicit labels and map context.

### five-g-slicing-card

5G slicing cards are business-facing: dedicated slice, guaranteed local performance, latency, throughput, site, and service level. Keep it technical and credible.

### private-network-card

Private mobile network cards show campus, factory, port, hospital, coverage, devices, SIMs, edge, and SLA. Use business proof.

### iot-connectivity-card

IoT cards show NB-IoT, LTE-M, 5G, devices, telemetry, roaming, battery, latency, and platform dashboard. Use network diagrams, not generic icons.

### business-dashboard-card

Business account cards show fleet devices, connectivity, bills, sites, usage, tickets, and contract actions. Keep controls dense.

### small-business-bundle-card

Small business bundles show broadband, mobile, landline, security, collaboration, and monthly price. Use practical copy.

### enterprise-proof-panel

Enterprise panels show analyst recognition, network scale, global IoT, private networks, and case studies. Pair claims with metrics or product proof.

### store-locator-card

Store cards show nearest shop, opening hours, appointment, device repair, and collection. Use map and simple route.

### comparison-table

Comparison tables show plan columns, data, speed, roaming, entertainment, contract, upfront, monthly, and annual increase note. Sticky headers help.

### checkout-summary-card

Checkout summaries show basket, device, plan, discounts, delivery, upfront, monthly, and legal terms. Make commitment clear.

### red-cta-band

Red CTA bands can close campaigns or push a deal. Use white type and a white or black button. Avoid red text on dark.

### footer-directory

Footer groups Mobile, Broadband, Business, Support, About, Careers, Privacy, Accessibility, stores, and social. Keep it functional.

## §2 Palette

Vodafone is red, white, black, and practical grays. Red is brand identity and action; it should not be diluted by extra accent colors.

#### vodafone-red

Use red for primary CTA, offer emphasis, brand bands, selected states, and campaign moments. It needs strong contrast and restraint.

#### white-retail

Use white for shopping, plans, comparison, account, support, and business utility surfaces.

#### black-voltage

Use black for high-impact hero or footer bands and premium product photography. White text on black should be large and clear.

#### gray-utility

Use grays for plan dividers, disabled controls, footnotes, input backgrounds, and comparison structure.

## §3 Typography

Vodafone typography is heavy, direct, and retail-friendly.

#### display-type

Headlines can be very large and heavy. Use sentence case or short campaign phrasing; avoid tiny delicate display type.

#### body-type

Body copy must explain offers, terms, speed, contract, and eligibility clearly.

#### price-type

Prices and speeds need strong hierarchy: monthly price, upfront cost, data allowance, Mbps, and contract length.

## §4 Composition Rules

Vodafone compositions must balance campaign impact with offer clarity.

### offer-first

Lead with plan, price, speed, data, or bundle benefit. Do not hide the commercial detail behind lifestyle imagery.

### red-with-purpose

Use red to guide purchase or campaign emphasis. Too much red makes comparison harder.

### device-plus-plan

When selling devices, always show plan context. The phone and tariff are inseparable.

### network-proof

Network claims need coverage, technology, performance, or availability evidence.

### terms-visible

Telecom terms are part of the product. Price rises, contract length, roaming zones, and eligibility must be visible.

## §5 Accessibility And States

Telecom pages are high-stakes retail flows. Contrast, focus, and legal clarity matter.

### focus-state

Use red or black focus rings with sufficient offset. On red backgrounds, use white/black focus treatment.

### availability-state

Availability states need labels: available, not available, check address, limited coverage, planned work, outage.

### pricing-state

Pricing states need monthly, upfront, discount, annual increase, and total due today labels.

### support-state

Support states should say diagnosing, booked, repaired, escalated, resolved, or action needed.

## §6 Anti-Patterns

Vodafone style fails when it becomes vague red marketing.

### avoid-red-wash

Do not fill every surface red. White plan comparison is essential.

### avoid-hidden-terms

Never bury contract length, price increases, speed limits, or roaming conditions.

### avoid-generic-tech

5G and IoT need network specifics, not abstract glowing city graphics.

### avoid-soft-consumer-pastels

Vodafone is bold retail telecom. Pastel SaaS cards weaken the brand.
