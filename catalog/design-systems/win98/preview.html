Surface elevation

Depth comes from the 4-value bevel and inset shadow stops. No soft drop shadows; no radius; the geometry tells the elevation story.

Outset (raised)

A raised surface — white top & left, gray bottom & right. Buttons, cards, badges, decorative squares.

Outset deep

The doubled bevel reads as a heavier hardware control. Reserved for prominent CTA cards and toolbar groups.

Inset (sunken)

Reversed border-color sinks the surface — gray top & left, white bottom & right. Content panes and form inputs live here.

Typography

Six roles, system fonts only. Display reaches for Arial Black or Impact; body falls to MS Sans Serif; monospace carries chrome (counters, dates, code).
