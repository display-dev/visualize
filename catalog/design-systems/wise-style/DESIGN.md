# Wise Style

_Independent reference inspired by Wise's public product, pricing, business, and platform surfaces. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Wise._

## §1 Canonical Canvas

Wise style is a bright global-money interface built around transparency. The current product story spans fast international transfers, multi-currency accounts, cards, receiving money, interest or assets features by market, Wise Business, Wise Platform, card issuing, APIs, transparent pricing, and regulated cross-border payment infrastructure.

Use lime green, sage surfaces, warm near-black ink, huge rounded headlines, currency controls, fee breakdowns, country and currency selectors, card visuals, trust/legal notes, and product modules that show exactly what happens to money. The brand should feel friendly, plainspoken, and specific.

### global-navigation

Navigation splits clearly into Personal, Business, and Platform. Menus expose send money, large transfers, receive money, card, interest or assets, team finances, accounting software, pricing, API integrations, and help.

### transfer-hero

The transfer hero should put an operational calculator beside the promise. Show send amount, recipient amount, currencies, arrival time, fees, discount or promotion state, and primary send action.

### currency-converter-card

Currency converter cards show amount input, source currency, destination currency, mid-market rate, total fee, recipient amount, and timing. Every number must be legible.

### route-selector

Route selectors show popular country pairs, flags, delivery method, estimated arrival, fee, and rate. Make global coverage visible without creating decorative flag wallpaper.

### account-overview-card

Multi-currency account cards show balances, 40 currencies, account details, receive-money actions, convert buttons, and spending card links.

### local-details-card

Local account detail cards show currency, routing information, IBAN or account number, copy actions, verification state, and receive limits when relevant.

### card-spending-panel

Card panels show Wise card, currency used, conversion rate, no foreign transaction charge claim where applicable, wallet support, ATM fee context, and freeze controls.

### business-dashboard

Business dashboards show payments, balances, team spending, approvals, employee cards, accounting integrations, recipients, invoices, and roles.

### team-card-controls

Team card controls show employee card, spending limit, payment methods, approval state, digital card, freeze action, and transaction notification.

### accounting-integration-card

Accounting cards show connected software, sync status, exported transactions, reconciliation, expense category, and owner. Keep it practical.

### transparent-pricing-table

Pricing tables show feature tabs: send, receive, convert, card, holding money, account details, ATM withdrawals, wire or Swift fees, and regional disclaimers.

### fee-breakdown-row

Fee rows should separate exchange rate, Wise fee, delivery fee if any, discount, recipient gets, and total paid. Hidden markup is the enemy of this style.

### mid-market-rate-callout

Mid-market callouts explain that Wise uses the fair exchange rate customers can check elsewhere. Keep the claim restrained and attached to actual calculator data.

### speed-proof-card

Speed proof cards show under-20-second payments, under-24-hour delivery, pending state, completed state, and route-dependent caveats.

### trust-safety-panel

Trust panels show privacy, 2FA, safeguarding, regulation, service status, support languages, legal entity, and bank or program-bank notes where relevant.

### interest-assets-card

Interest or assets cards must be market-specific. Show APY or return only with date, eligibility, risk/legal note, access behavior, and product entity.

### platform-hero

Platform pages shift from consumer friendliness to infrastructure. Show banks, financial institutions, enterprises, payment solutions, and the phrase that the network powers the world's money.

### platform-metrics-strip

Platform metric strips show FX volume, payment speed, country coverage, currency coverage, licenses, STP rate, and engineering or support depth.

### api-product-card

API cards show Send, Receive, Issue Cards, Multi-currency Accounts, docs, guides, API reference, changelog, auth, sandbox, and support.

### embedded-payout-flow

Embedded payout flows show customer onboarding, KYC, recipient, payment route, fee, compliance check, payout status, webhook, and reconciliation.

### partner-industry-grid

Industry grids show banks, education platforms, marketplaces, spend management, travel platforms, and workforce platforms with specific payment jobs.

### wise-connect-card

Wise Connect cards show conference city, date, registration, platform topics, cross-border payments, and product announcements.

### support-help-panel

Help panels show search, contact support, service status, complaints, country site map, legal, privacy, and accessibility.

### footer-regulatory-block

Footer blocks should include legal entity, authorization language, card issuer notes, investment service notes, fees-vary disclaimers, and country availability.

## §2 Palette

Wise color is lime green plus warm financial neutrals. The palette should feel fresh, not banking-blue.

#### wise-lime

Use vivid lime for primary CTAs, key conversion states, success moments, and brand stamps. It must stay readable with dark ink.

#### sage-canvas

Use pale sage for hero bands, calculator surrounds, account modules, and quiet product sections.

#### warm-ink

Use warm near-black with a slight olive cast for headings, controls, and body text.

#### money-status-colors

Use green for positive, yellow for warning, red for negative, and cyan sparingly for informational states. Status color must carry labels too.

#### dark-app-canvas

Use warm near-black for app-dark panels, platform proof blocks, and dark-mode previews, keeping lime as the constant brand action.

## §3 Typography

Wise typography is chunky, friendly, and operationally clear.

#### heavy-wise-display

Use an extra-heavy rounded display sans for major headlines. Large, compact headlines are part of the brand voice.

#### neutral-product-sans

Use a neutral sans for forms, tables, calculators, buttons, and legal copy. Product data needs calm readability.

#### numeric-clarity

Amounts, rates, fees, and arrival times need tabular rhythm, generous spacing, and visible currency codes.

#### plainspoken-labels

Labels should sound practical: You send, Recipient gets, Total fees, Arrives, Convert, Account details, Open an account.

## §4 Composition Rules

Wise layouts should prove the promise by showing the transaction mechanics.

### calculator-first

Lead important pages with a calculator, fee table, account balance, card, or payment flow. Avoid abstract fintech illustration as the main proof.

### show-the-fee

Expose rates, fees, and arrival time in the same component as the action. Transparency is not a footnote.

### rounded-but-useful

Use large rounded cards and pill buttons, but keep controls dense enough for real financial comparison.

### global-but-specific

Show countries, currencies, routes, and local details. Global coverage must be concrete.

### legal-near-claim

Put regulatory, risk, availability, and eligibility notes near the claims they qualify.

### business-and-platform-split

Separate Business workflow UI from Platform infrastructure UI. One is an account for teams; the other is embedded payment rails.

### calm-proof

Use metrics and customer numbers plainly. Avoid hype charts, crypto energy, or speculative finance language.

## §5 Accessibility And States

Money UI needs exceptionally clear state design.

### focus-state

Use lime or dark focus rings with strong contrast on sage, white, and dark app canvases.

### transfer-state

Transfer states include quoted, funded, verifying, sending, arrived, delayed, cancelled, refunded, and failed.

### verification-state

Verification states include unverified, ID required, reviewing, approved, blocked, and additional info needed.

### fee-state

Fee states include free, discounted, fixed fee, percentage fee, volume discount, operator fee, and unavailable route.

### card-state

Card states include active, frozen, digital, physical ordered, shipped, replaced, expired, limit reached, and declined.

## §6 Anti-Patterns

Wise style fails when it becomes vague fintech optimism.

### avoid-hidden-fees

Never hide fees, rates, or arrival times behind marketing copy.

### avoid-bank-blue

Do not default to navy, royal blue, or conservative bank gradients. Lime and sage carry the brand.

### avoid-speculative-wealth

Do not frame Wise like investing, crypto, or wealth management unless a market-specific assets product is explicitly in scope.

### avoid-flag-confetti

Flags are useful selectors, not decoration. Use them only where country or route choice matters.
