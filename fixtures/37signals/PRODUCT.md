# Brand · 37signals

## What this is

A small, profitable, opinionated software company that has shipped Basecamp (project management) and HEY (email) for 25 years without taking outside investment. Also publishes Shape Up (project methodology), Rework (book), It Doesn't Have to Be Crazy at Work (book), and a steady stream of opinionated manifesto-shape essays via Jason Fried and DHH. The company brand is heavier than either product's brand.

## Voice

**Axis: raw / opinionated** (manifesto register).

Short sentences. Direct claims. Position-taking is the default mode — on hiring, on funding, on remote work, on management, on tools, on the industry's whole shape. Hedging is rare and deliberate; when 37signals hedges, the position matters. No corporate-speak, no "trusted by", no logo walls of customer brands, no "transforming the way teams work" prose.

When `voice` choices arise (Postmortem reflection, FAQ phrasings, Plan-review prose): keep the edge. The brand's value is the position; sand it off and you have a generic productivity company.

## Audience

- **Primary**: small-team founders, indie developers, anti-VC startup builders, opinionated tech leads.
- **Secondary**: large-company employees reading 37signals as counter-programming to their own org's culture.

The primary audience reads on the 37signals.com blog, on `world.hey.com`, on DHH's Twitter, in the books, and in newsletters. They expect the brand to *have an opinion* — neutral phrasing reads as betrayal.

## Format default

**presentation** — most artifacts are presentation-shaped: manifestos, conference keynotes, book excerpts, opinionated essay-as-page. The company also ships product-shaped artifacts (changelog, status, postmortems) but those carry the same voice.

The theme-pick algorithm routes `presentation` + `raw` → Brutalist, which lines up — 37signals.com is famously bare HTML with strong typography, hard edges, and no marketing chrome.

## Exceptions

`slop/uppercase-body` is whitelisted for short manifestos and titles where caps land the register (DHH's `IT'S BASECAMP` style). The detector should not "fix" these. `slop/wide-prose` is whitelisted on book-excerpt pages — long reading-measure is part of the publishing-register voice for those surfaces.
