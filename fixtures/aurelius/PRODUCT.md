# Brand · Aurelius Institute

## What this is

A small not-for-profit research institute publishing peer-reviewed working papers on macroeconomics, climate finance, and labour-market structure. Audience reads on the institute's website and on academic-search aggregators (SSRN, RePEc). Outputs include working papers, policy briefs, dataset releases, and methodology notes.

## Voice

**Axis: institutional** (academic register).

Sober, hedged where the data hedges, citation-heavy. Sentences are longer than dev-tool register; passive voice is acceptable where it sharpens the methodology. No "we are crushing it" / "huge win" / "game-changer" — the prose carries trust through restraint and disclosure, not hype.

When `voice` choices arise (e.g. softening cuts in a Plan review, framing a Postmortem's reflection): lean toward neutral-formal, never colloquial.

## Audience

- **Primary**: peer researchers, policy staff at central banks + treasuries, academic peer-reviewers.
- **Secondary**: financial journalists citing the institute as a source, graduate students.

The primary audience reads slowly, scans bibliographies, and cares whether the methodology is reproducible. They do not need (and resent) marketing chrome.

## Format default

**publishing** — most artifacts are prose-led long-form documents (working papers, briefs). The institute also issues quarterly programme reviews (Plan-review shape) and methodology FAQs.

## Exceptions

None at launch. The genre-reflex check applies strictly: institutional register is *especially* prone to academic-paper cliché (two-column equations layout, all-greyscale, JSTOR-as-aesthetic). Earn the formality through type and disclosure, not through dressing-up.
