The press doesn’t need permission

Halcyon’s zine team prints a weekly issue on the same risograph the engineering room uses for spec drafts. The crimson plate runs first, the charcoal text block lands second, and the script overlay goes on by brush. Nothing is centred. Nothing is symmetrical.
