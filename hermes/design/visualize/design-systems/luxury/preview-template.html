Stillwater & Vale Annual report · MMXXVI

Letter from the chairman

The year we chose patience.

A note on the discipline of refusing the obvious moves, the value compounded by saying no to the wrong opportunities, and what the firm has learned in its twenty-second year.

Funds under management
$4.82B

Across nine vintages spanning twenty-two years; deployed with the same partner since inception.

Net realised IRR · cohort 2014–2024
28.4%

Top decile against peer benchmarks. Net of fees, net of carry, net of every excuse.

We did not chase the conferences. We did not enter the league tables. We declined seventeen of every twenty opportunities the year presented us — and the three that remained have, individually, returned the fund.
