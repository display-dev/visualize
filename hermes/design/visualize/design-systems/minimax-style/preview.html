Tokens · Surfaces · Five steps

Canvas through ink, scarce dark band at the closer.

Pages run on canvas, lift to surface for documentation backgrounds, return to card for content tiles, accept ink only at the action moment, and close on the dark footer band. The ink step is the brand's commitment voltage; the footer band is the scarce-dark register.

01 · Canvas --background page floor · marketing body · cards on white
02 · Surface --brand-surface search-pill rest · sidebar active · soft section
03 · Card --card documentation card · pricing tier · table row
04 · Ink pill --primary dominant CTA · "commit here" voltage
05 · Footer band --brand-footer-bg promo banner · multi-column footer · closer

Tokens · Type + components

DM Sans across every role, tight 1.10 leading at the display.

Hero displays run DM Sans 600 with -0.04em letter-spacing and 1.10 line-height. Body settles at 16px, 1.5 leading. Buttons are universally pill-shaped, badges sit in a pill register, and the segmented pill-tab group carries the brand's documented documentation nav.
