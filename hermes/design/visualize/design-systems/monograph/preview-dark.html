monograph

A printed register for slow thinking on cream paper. Cover stock weighs 280 gsm; the inside pages are uncoated, milled in Småland, and bound by hand. One ink colour shouts. Two more sit quietly behind it.

Three accents, one cream.

The cream is the page. The red-orange is the only ink that talks at body size. Warm orange and soft pink stay in the blob composition; the paper holds the temperature.

Elevation by paper weight.

The register rejects drop shadows and rounded corners. The ladder runs across three near-identical cream tints; spatial separation is whitespace and scale.

surface · canvas

The page itself.

Body copy sits here at default body weight. The gradient blob composition assumes cream beneath; substituting white turns the multiply blend into a digital wash.

surface · paper-soft

A breath above.

A barely-perceptible lift for pull-quote slabs and campaign blocks. The card has no border, no shadow; the tint alone signals containment.

surface · paper-strong

Muted-tier.

For chip backgrounds, eyebrow strips, and the rare bordered block. Still cream, still warm — the chroma never leaves the page.

Six roles.

Clash Display compressed to leading 0.75 carries the poster. Satoshi at 1.55 leading carries the page. Everything else is restraint.
