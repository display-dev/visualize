Workspace · Long Read

The whole-team room, ported from a 1990s newsroom.

What product teams can borrow from how an editorial floor stages the day around a single editorial calendar.

by Jordan Kim · 9 min read · May 24, 2026

The newsroom of the late nineties had a quality that most product teams lost when they moved to chat. Every desk in the room could see the whiteboard, and every conversation that mattered happened in front of it — assigning a piece, killing a piece, debating a kicker.

Halcyon's editorial canvas is an attempt to restore that whiteboard. Notes are drafts, reviews are thread, the calendar is the surface — and the room can see the calendar without anyone having to ping anyone.
