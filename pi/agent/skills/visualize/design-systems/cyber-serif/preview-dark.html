Signature surface

Three pieces that compose, one at a time.

The grid below stacks the three named components from the spec: a glassmorphism card as the base, a shimmer border travelling at 4s linear, and a 600px spotlight on the cursor that fades in on hover. Move the pointer across any card to see the radial gradient pick it up.

A faint lift over the canvas.

The glassmorphism base sits at two-percent white with a one-pixel hairline at ten-percent. Backdrop blur reads the field behind it without ever overwhelming the canvas.

A pulse of emerald, sweeping.

Three colour stops in a horizontal gradient at two-hundred percent width, animating its position from positive to negative across four seconds. The eye reads it as light travelling around the edge.

Radial gradient at six-hundred pixels, fifteen-percent emerald at the centre fading to transparent at forty-percent radius. The hover transition lifts opacity from zero to one over three-eighty milliseconds.

Typography

Serif sets the register.

Newsreader at display, Inter at body, Space Grotesk at the smallest technical labels. Six roles in service of one tonal claim — editorial gravitas, with a high-tech caption layer underneath.

Display Editorial, sharpened.
Heading A title that earns its line height.
Title Section title in the same serif.
Body Body text in Inter at light weight. Long passages stay readable on the near-black canvas because the foreground sits at near-white, the line height stays at one-point-five, and the measure never stretches beyond fifty-six characters.
Caption A caption in Inter at thirteen pixels, used under figures and inside meta rows.
Mono / label Space Grotesk · 10px / 0.2em
