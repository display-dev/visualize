halcyon · engineering

Run models on the same laptop as the editor.

Halcyon's engineering team serves the small models locally and reaches for the big ones over the network only when the task asks for it. The CLI is the whole product surface.

tokens · surfaces

Three surfaces. Used sparingly.

The paper-white canvas does most of the work. The soft surface lifts only where a pill or a tile needs to read as a click target. The inverted dark moment appears exactly once per page.
