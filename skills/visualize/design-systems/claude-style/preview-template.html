Tokens · Typography

Slab-serif display, humanist body.

Copernicus (Tiempos Headline / Source Serif 4) carries display at weight 400 with negative tracking. StyreneB (Inter) carries body sans. JetBrains Mono for code. Literary editorial voice over technical SaaS.
