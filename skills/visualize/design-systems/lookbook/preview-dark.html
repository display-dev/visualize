A note from the studio

We started cataloguing the things in the studio for ourselves. A coat that survived four winters of darkroom work. A cup that held coffee through every print run since 2022. A notebook that kept track of every roll of film we developed in the spring of last year.

The catalogue is not a shop. It is a record of the objects we trust enough to use every day. Each entry is photographed in the room it lives in, dated to the week it joined the working set, and priced at the cost of replacing it.
