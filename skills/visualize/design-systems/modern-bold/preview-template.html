Typography is the visual language.

Bold Typography is poster design translated to web. Color exists to create contrast, space exists to frame letterforms, and interaction reveals typographic detail. Halcyon's design team uses this surface for its yearly brand monograph.

Tokens · Colour

Two surfaces and one accent.

Near-black canvas, warm white ink, vermillion accent. Black isn't pure (#0A0A0A); white isn't sterile (#FAFAFA). The accent is warm, urgent, visible — used on headlines and underlines only.

--background Canvas · #0A0A0A
--foreground Ink · #FAFAFA
--primary Accent · #FF3D00
--muted Lift · #1A1A1A
--muted-foreground Body mute · #737373
--border Hairline · #262626

Tokens · Surface

No shadows. Lift comes from layering.

Three steps inside the dark canvas, then one flip to the inverted plate. Hairline borders mark every transition. Depth is carried by typography and dividers, never by drop shadow.

Tokens · Typography

Three families, strict role separation.

Inter Tight carries UI and headlines with tighter default tracking. Playfair Display appears only in pull quotes and testimonials. JetBrains Mono runs all labels, stats, and technical captions.

Vocabulary · Components

Sharp edges, layered depth, one chromatic hue.

Zero radius throughout. Buttons carry intent through underline and inversion, not through fill. Cards are transparent with hairline borders. Inputs sit on the muted lift, focus to the accent.

Plate 02

The grid that breathes.

Asymmetric splits — 7/5 or 8/4 — let display type carry the visual weight while the body column rests at readable width.

Plate 03

Underline as affordance.

The animated underline scales past the text on hover. Interaction is revealed in the type itself, not in a new container.

Plate 04

Negative space is the frame.

Generous margins make headlines feel intentional. The frame around the type is half of the design — never cramped, never optional.
