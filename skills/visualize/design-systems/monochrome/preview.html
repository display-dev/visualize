§05 · Typography

Three families, six working roles.

DisplayPlayfair Display · 400 · 9–10rem · tracking -0.05em
Margins, quietly
HeadingPlayfair Display · 600 · 3–4rem · tracking -0.025em
The page is the entire argument
TitlePlayfair Display · 600 · 1.75rem · tracking -0.015em
A section needs a name, not a graphic
BodySource Serif 4 · 400 · 1.125rem · leading 1.625
Body text reads at the same lightness as the headings. Differentiation is carried by weight, by size, and by the rules above and below — not by lifting the type into a softer grey, because softer grey is a step toward the chromatic palette this system refuses to keep.
CaptionSource Serif 4 italic · 400 · 1rem · muted
Italic small body for asides, byline meta, footnote text, and figure captions. The italics in Source Serif 4 are the second most-used face in the system.

§06 · Components

Buttons, fields, chips. No radius, no shadow, no second colour.

Primary button

Ink fill, instant inversion on hover.

Black fill, white text, uppercase mono label, generous padding, 100 ms transition. Hover inverts to white fill with a 2 px black border. Focus-visible outline at 3 px, offset 3 px.

Secondary button

Transparent fill, 2 px ink border.

The outline variant. Inverts to the primary register on hover — both buttons land at the same destination state, so a secondary placed beside a primary in a row reads as a deliberate choice, not a hierarchy slip.

Ghost button

Reads as a text link.

No border, no background. Underline appears on hover (instant). For inline interactive language inside body copy where the primary or secondary register would read as too heavy.

Input field

Border thickens on focus — no coloured ring.

2 px ink border at rest, thickens to 4 px on focus. Italic placeholder at --muted-foreground. The border-weight change is the entire focus indicator — no outline ring, no underline, no halo.

Chip · label

Bordered or solid. Always mono, always tracking-widest.

Volume 09 Bound Quarterly

Sharp-cornered, no fill or solid black. The solid variant is the inversion-as-chip — same shape, opposite polarity, used for status that needs to feel decided.

Pull quote

Italic serif, oversized quotation marks.

The page is the argument. Everything else is the room the page is being read in. — Mira Allende, Issue 09

Title-scale Playfair Display italic, flanked by 8rem decorative quotation marks at 15 % opacity. Top and bottom 1 px rules bracket the quote.
