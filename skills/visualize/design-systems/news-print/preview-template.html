The Surfaces

Page A3 · Hierarchy
Surface 0 · Paper
Newsprint canvas.

The default body fill. Warm off-white, dot-grid pattern at four-percent opacity, the substrate every other surface sits on.

Surface 1 · Line texture
Section paper.

Major sections layer a three-pixel graph-paper crosshatch over Surface 1 fill. Fine, quiet, never decorative for its own sake.

Surface 2 · Halftone
Photograph treatment.

Image placeholders render the sixteen-pixel halftone screen at ten-percent opacity. Stand-in for the morning's grayscale photography.

Editor's note

How the morning edition assembles itself.

01

Files arrive overnight.

Correspondents wire copy to the assignment desk by 04:30. The wire is two paragraphs of context and three paragraphs of detail, in that order.

02

The grid is set first.

Page A1 gets the column structure before any headline lands. Lead story claims eight columns; the sidebar takes the remaining four without negotiation.

03

Letters close the loop.

The final spread carries Letters to the Editor and the imprint. Every reader response gets a numbered reference; resolutions appear in the next issue.
