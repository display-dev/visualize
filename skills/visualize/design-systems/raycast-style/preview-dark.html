Tokens · Surfaces

Four-step surface ladder.

Canvas at the floor. Surface lift for the palette frame. Surface-elevated for active rows and contextual chrome. Card surface for the deepest contained UI. Depth is carried by surface lift + 1px hairline borders — never by drop shadows.

Floor --background page canvas · near-black
Lift 1 --brand-surface palette frame · footer rail
Lift 2 --brand-surface-elevated active row · chip
Card --card popover · deepest contained
