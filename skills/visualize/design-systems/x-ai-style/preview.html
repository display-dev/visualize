001 · Model surface

A frontier-research card on a charcoal lift.

The single signature mockup. Three cards on canvas-card with a glyph tile, a Universal-Sans name, a quiet body line, and a mono identifier strip across the bottom border. Accent tones (sunset, dusk, breeze) surface here only — never as canvas fill.

Atlas Reason

The default research surface. Long-horizon planning, tool calls in a deterministic order, no truncation across multi-step engineering sessions.

002 · Tokens · Colour

Near-black canvas, restrained accents.

Canvas is one shade. Cards lift by ~6 percentage points of lightness. The accent palette — sunset orange, dusk purple, twilight violet, breeze blue — sits in the tokens as illustrative ink, never as a canvas fill.

003 · Tokens · Surface

Four lifts on canvas, no shadows.

Depth on the dark canvas is read through surface lift and a single hairline border. Drop-shadow is absent from the brand. Canvas-mid is reserved for nested elements — code mockups, glyph tiles, secondary chrome inside cards.

Floor · Canvas --background Page surface, hero, footer.
Lift 1 --brand-canvas-soft Nav hover, tooltip, input.
Lift 2 --brand-canvas-card Cards, tier cards, tag pills.
Lift 3 --brand-canvas-mid Glyph tiles, nested chrome.

004 · Tokens · Typography

Display tracks aggressively negative.

Universal Sans (or Inter as the open substitute) carries every display at weight 400. Negative letter-spacing at hero scale gives the typography its precision feel. Geist Mono uppercase pairs as the section eyebrow — the contrast is the brand voice.

Display · XL Halcyon.
Display · LG Built for teams that ship.
Display · MD Token surface preview rendered against this design system.
Display · SM Card-cluster heading scale.
Body · MD Default paragraph copy at 16px on the dark canvas, line-height 1.55, letter-spacing about -0.05px. Body weight is 400 like everything else; emphasis on this surface is carried by size and tracking, not by bolding.

005 · Vocabulary · Components

One button shape, one card shape.

The outline pill is universal. Cards are 8px rectangles with a 1px hairline border. The exception is the white-filled Sign-up pill — and the brand uses that exactly once.
