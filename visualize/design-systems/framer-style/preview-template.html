Tokens · Surfaces

Three dark registers.

Near-pure black canvas as the page floor. Slightly lifted surface-1 for charcoal cards and pricing tiles. Stronger surface-2 for emphasis blocks. Gradients sit on top of the lifted surfaces, never directly on the canvas.
