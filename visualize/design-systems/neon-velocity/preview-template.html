01/Ethos

Halcyon's engineering team ships against a single near-black canvas and a single chromatic accent. Constraints set velocity. Every surface earns its place in the system before it touches a customer.

Hold the line

02/Method

Brutalist mass over glass depth. Twelve-pixel sharp hero frame against two-rem rounded cards below. The mixed-radius contrast is the system's identity, not a coincidence — it carries the page's hierarchy on its own.

Sharp and round

03/Tempo

Tabular digits hold their column on every tick. The countdown never reflows, the deploy timestamps never pulse, the stat band never twitches. Pace is a visual property before it's a runtime one.

No reflow
