Stable Grid, Wild Decoration

Tactile fun, on a readable grid.

A design system that puts the energy in the gutters and keeps the content quiet. Chunky borders, hard offset shadows, a warm cream canvas, and three decorative tones rotating through the page. Halcyon's design team uses it for marketing pages, internal portals, and weekend side projects.

Signature · Pop Shadow primitive

Rest, lift, pressed — one primitive, three positions.

The defining surface treatment is a hard offset shadow with zero blur. Every interactive primitive — buttons, cards, focused inputs — sits on the same three-state ladder. The shadow's position is what animates; the colour stays put.

01 · Rest

The page at rest.

Shadow at 4px 4px 0 0. The primitive sits two notches off the page — far enough to read as raised, close enough to feel pressed against the canvas.

Workflows

Weekly rituals — sprint reviews, design crits, retro notes. The card sits where the page expects it to sit.

02 · Lift

Hover finds the primitive.

The surface translates -2/-2 and the shadow extends to 6/6. The whole motion is one bouncy cubic-bezier — overshoot built in, no spring physics required.

Featured workflow

Pink-shadow variant — the sticker-card register the spec uses for the featured pricing tier and any card the page wants to flag.

03 · Pressed

Click compresses the offset.

On press the surface moves +2/+2 and the shadow shrinks to 2/2. The primitive looks pinned to the page for the duration of the press, then bounces back when released.

New workflow

The icon plate uses mint for success, amber for warning, pink for new — never floating alone on the canvas.

Tokens · Colour

One violet primary, three rotational tones.

Violet carries every primary action. Hot pink, amber, and mint rotate as decorative — the spec is explicit that they're not state colours. Body type runs slate-800 on a warm cream canvas; pure black never appears in the system.

Tokens · Elevation · Offset ladder

0, 2, 4, 6, 8 — the whole elevation language.

Drop-shadows with blur never appear. The system's elevation is a five-step ladder of hard offset, zero blur. The first step is flush; the last is the featured-card register.

00 · Flush 0 0 0 0 inline label, table cell, helper meta
01 · Pressed 2 2 0 ink primitive on click, compressed offset
02 · Rest 4 4 0 ink Candy Button default, focused input
03 · Lift 6 6 0 ink primitive on hover, extended offset
04 · Card 8 8 0 border Sticker Card default and featured variants

Tokens · Type + components

Outfit for display, Plus Jakarta for everything else.

Two families, a 1.25 major-third scale, and chunky display weights. Outfit at 700 / 800 carries every headline; Plus Jakarta Sans at 400 / 500 carries body and UI. Mono falls back to the system stack — the spec doesn't enumerate a brand mono.
