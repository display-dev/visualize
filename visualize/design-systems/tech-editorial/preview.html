§ Palette · 06 tokens

A muted ground, an honest ink, one accent kept in reserve.

The system runs on paper warmth — never bright white, never pure black. A single forest-green primary carries every call to action; the scan-blue stays inside its progress track and nowhere else.

§ Elevation

Hairlines, not shadows.

Every surface separates by a one-pixel rule. No drop shadow, no embossed lift — the register's structural honesty refuses the easy elevation.

§ Components

The shapes a brutalist magazine needs.

Buttons, badges, bottom-bordered inputs, hairline-framed cards, and a tab strip cut from a single rectangle. The system declines to invent decoration where a rule and a typeface will do.
