Tokens · Surface

Four surfaces. No in-between greys.

The brand alternates aggressively between a white canvas and a near-black navy band. A soft canvas-grey carries table-header rows; a slightly-lifted dark soft carries on-dark cards. The dark-light contrast does the elevation work that drop-shadows would otherwise.

Tokens · Typography

Two faces. The brand's tonal joke.

A custom geometric display sans (extracted as The Future, Inter substitute) carries every headline and body. An uppercase mono caption face (PP Neue Montreal Mono, JetBrains Mono substitute) labels every section, every button, every table-header cell. Headlines stay sentence-case; the all-caps moments belong only to the mono.
