tokens · surfaces

Three surfaces, one canvas.

The warm charcoal canvas is the floor. Canvas-soft sits a step above for workflow-block bodies and feature cards. The white pill is the only inverse moment — used sparingly, never as a fill on chrome.

Inverse --primary single white pill · featured tier · primary CTA
